How it starts and stops
- start: builds the tests image cbi-tests:<hash of its Dockerfile and lockfile> if it is missing (once, shared by every run).
  Then it builds the product image from the checkout (product.Dockerfile: node:22-bookworm-slim, ca-certificates, corepack's
  pnpm, pnpm install --frozen-lockfile with a BuildKit cache, then the source) and brings the product up with
  "compose up --wait". The product is ready by its healthcheck (GET / answers below 500, every 2 s, up to 5 min), never a
  fixed sleep. Dependencies are fetched only when a lockfile changes, in the image build, never by a run. A product that
  dies at start makes start: exit 1.
- With NO_PRODUCT=1, start: builds nothing of the product and starts nothing; only the tests image is made ready.
- stop: compose down with volumes, orphans and the run's product image, then the leftovers check; exit 1 if anything of
  cbi-<RUN_ID> remains. A stop with nothing up exits 0.
- services: prints "product died, state exited, exit <code>" for a product that ended other than with 0.
- logs: the Compose logs with times, then each service's end state.
